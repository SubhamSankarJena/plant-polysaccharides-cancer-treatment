# Project Information

## Project Title

**Plant Polysaccharides in Cancer Treatment**

## Project Type

Academic Literature Review / Project Report

## Domain

Pharmaceutical Sciences • Cancer Research • Natural Products • Pharmacology

## Primary Focus

This project reviews the potential role of plant polysaccharides in cancer research and treatment, with emphasis on their reported biological activities, mechanisms of action, immunomodulatory effects, anti-tumor activity, combination therapy, and emerging delivery systems.

## Key Topics

- Plant polysaccharides
- Cancer biology
- Natural products
- Immunomodulation
- Anti-tumor activity
- Apoptosis
- Cell-cycle regulation
- Anti-angiogenesis
- Antioxidant activity
- Combination therapy
- Nanoparticles
- Liposomes
- Hydrogels
- Drug delivery
