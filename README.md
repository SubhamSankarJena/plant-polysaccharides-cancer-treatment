📌 Overview
This repository contains an academic review project focused on the potential role of plant polysaccharides in cancer treatment.
Plant polysaccharides are naturally occurring complex carbohydrates composed of monosaccharide units linked through glycosidic bonds. The project examines their reported biological activities and potential applications in cancer research, with emphasis on immunomodulation, anti-tumor activity, apoptosis, cell-cycle regulation, anti-angiogenesis, antioxidant activity, and combination therapy.
The report also discusses recent developments in polysaccharide-based delivery systems, selected polysaccharides, current challenges, and future research directions.
> **Scope:** This repository presents an academic literature-review project. It is not medical advice and does not establish plant polysaccharides as substitutes for conventional cancer treatment.
---
🎯 Objectives
The project addresses six major objectives:
Immunomodulation — Investigate the ability of plant polysaccharides to modulate immune responses involving macrophages, natural killer (NK) cells, and T-cells.
Anti-Tumor Activity — Review reported effects including apoptosis, cell-cycle arrest, and inhibition of angiogenesis.
Combination Therapy — Explore research on combining plant polysaccharides with conventional chemotherapeutic drugs.
Safety and Efficacy — Discuss safety, efficacy, pharmacokinetics, bioavailability, and potential toxicity considerations.
Mechanistic Studies — Examine proposed interactions with cellular pathways, signaling mechanisms, and the tumor microenvironment.
Novel Therapeutics — Review polysaccharide-based formulations and advanced delivery approaches.

⚠️ Challenges & Limitations
The project identifies several challenges:
Standardization: Variation in composition between sources can influence biological activity and reproducibility.
Bioavailability: Limited oral bioavailability is identified as a concern for some polysaccharides.
Dose Optimization: Further research is needed to determine appropriate doses.
Mechanistic Understanding: Molecular mechanisms underlying reported effects require further investigation.
Clinical Validation: Rigorous clinical studies are needed to establish safety and efficacy.
Advanced Delivery: Nanoparticle and other delivery systems are being explored to improve stability and bioavailability.
