## 📌 Overview

**Plant Polysaccharides in Cancer Treatment** is an academic literature-review project exploring the potential role of naturally occurring polysaccharides in cancer research and therapeutic development.

Plant polysaccharides are complex carbohydrates composed of monosaccharide units connected through glycosidic bonds. They are widely distributed in plants and have been investigated for a range of biological activities.

This project reviews their reported biological properties and examines their potential relevance to cancer research, with particular emphasis on:

- 🛡️ Immunomodulation
- 🎯 Anti-tumor activity
- 🧬 Apoptosis
- 🔄 Cell-cycle regulation
- 🩸 Anti-angiogenesis
- 🧪 Antioxidant activity
- 🔥 Anti-inflammatory activity
- 💊 Combination therapy
- 🚚 Drug-delivery systems
- 🧫 Tumor microenvironment
- 🦠 Gut microbiota interactions

The project also examines selected polysaccharides such as **β-glucans, pectins, arabinogalactans, fucoidans, and inulin**, together with emerging approaches involving nanoparticles, liposomes, and hydrogels.

> **Scope:** This repository contains an academic literature review. It is intended for educational and research purposes and should not be interpreted as medical advice or as evidence that plant polysaccharides can replace established cancer treatments.

# 🧬 What Are Plant Polysaccharides?

Polysaccharides are high-molecular-weight carbohydrates consisting of monosaccharide units linked through glycosidic bonds.

Plant-derived polysaccharides are widely distributed throughout plant tissues and contribute to structural, storage, and functional roles.

The project discusses several major categories:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Cellulose** | Major structural polysaccharide of plant cell walls |
| **Hemicellulose** | Heterogeneous group of polysaccharides contributing to plant-cell-wall structure |
| **Pectin** | Polysaccharide rich in galacturonic acid |
| **Starch** | Major plant storage polysaccharide composed primarily of amylose and amylopectin |
| **Gums & Mucilages** | Plant-derived polysaccharides with applications in pharmaceutical, food, and other formulations |

Their structural diversity contributes to differences in their physicochemical properties and biological activities.

# 🔬 Biological Activities

Plant polysaccharides have been investigated for several biological activities.

## 🛡️ Immunomodulatory Activity

One of the major areas discussed in the project is immunomodulation.

Reported research has examined potential effects on:

- Macrophages
- Natural killer cells
- T-cells
- Other components of the immune response

The project particularly discusses macrophage polarization and the proposed relationship between different macrophage phenotypes and tumor progression.

---

## 🎯 Anti-Tumor Activity

The project reviews reported anti-tumor effects associated with selected polysaccharides.

Proposed effects include:

- Cell-cycle arrest
- Apoptosis
- Inhibition of tumor-cell proliferation
- Anti-angiogenesis
- Potential inhibition of invasion
- Potential effects on metastasis

These effects are discussed in the context of the literature reviewed in the project.

---

## 🧪 Antioxidant Activity

Some plant polysaccharides have been reported to exhibit antioxidant properties.

Potential activities discussed include:

- Free-radical scavenging
- Reduction of oxidative damage
- Modulation of oxidative stress

Oxidative stress is relevant to several biological processes, making antioxidant activity an area of interest in natural-product research.

---

## 🔥 Anti-Inflammatory Activity

The project also discusses reported anti-inflammatory effects.

Plant polysaccharides may influence inflammatory and immune responses, which are relevant to the tumor microenvironment and cancer biology.

---

## 🦠 Antimicrobial Activity

Selected plant polysaccharides have also been investigated for antimicrobial effects against microorganisms such as:

- Bacteria
- Fungi

This activity represents one of the broader biological properties associated with plant polysaccharides.

---

## 🩸 Antidiabetic Activity

The project additionally discusses reported effects related to:

- Blood-glucose regulation
- Insulin sensitivity
- Metabolic processes

These activities demonstrate the broader biological relevance of plant polysaccharides beyond cancer research.
